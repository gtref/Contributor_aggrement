# Adoption Instructions

To adopt the Contributor Agreement in your open-source project, follow these steps:

1. Link to the canonical version of the Contributor Agreement in your project's `CONTRIBUTING.md` file.
2. Direct contributors to review and agree to the terms prior to submitting pull requests.

Canonical link: [https://gtref.github.io/Contributor-agreement/v1.0](https://gtref.github.io/Contributor-agreement/v1.0.html)
