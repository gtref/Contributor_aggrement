# Introducing Contributor Agreement v1.0

## Summary
Contributor Agreement v1.0 is a standardized, versioned agreement designed for open-source software projects. It establishes clear operational guidelines and conduct expectations for contributors and maintainers, helping communities collaborate efficiently without legal ambiguity or unnecessary overhead.

## What’s in v1.0
- **Expectations:** Guidance on professional conduct, constructive communication, code standard compliance, and contribution rights.
- **Unacceptable Behavior:** Explicit prohibitions against harassment, discrimination, trolling, spamming, and malicious submissions.
- **Rights:** Clarification on copyright ownership, project licensing, and contributor privacy options.
- **Maintainer Responsibilities:** Commitments to prompt code reviews, actionable feedback, and transparent, impartial enforcement.
- **Contacting Maintainers:** Designated escalation channels for privacy concerns, conduct reports, and clarifications.
- **Enforcement:** A tiered resolution process ranging from private clarification to permanent bans and contribution removals.

## Why It Matters
Clear guidelines reduce friction in open-source maintainership. By defining mutual expectations and response procedures upfront, Contributor Agreement v1.0 fosters a safe, transparent, and productive development environment where contributors can focus on building quality software.

## How to Adopt It
Add the following snippet to your project's `README.md` or `CONTRIBUTING.md`:

```markdown
## Contributor Agreement
By contributing to this project, you agree to abide by the [Contributor Agreement v1.0](https://gtref.github.io/Contributor_aggrement/v1.0.html).
```
